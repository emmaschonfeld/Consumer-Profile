Orbit privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Orbit is an educational consumer intelligence laboratory.

Data you choose

With explicit permission, the connector reads Chrome URLs, page titles and visit timestamps for your chosen period. Imported JSON/CSV may contain browsing, user-authored conversations, orders or product catalogs. The connector only sends data to the authorized Orbit dashboard tab; it does not send it to a developer server. It never reads cookies, passwords, authentication tokens or other credentials.

Local analysis

Records and inferred profiles remain in dashboard tab memory. Reloading clears them. The extension ID alone is saved in localStorage. No external AI, tracking, sale, advertising, background collection or persistent raw-record storage. Static hosting can keep normal request logs when serving public assets; records are not part of those requests.

Your control

Delete records or sources in Connections & privacy. Disconnect revokes Chrome history permission and removes imported Chrome records. The toolbar popup can also revoke permission. Remove the extension in chrome://extensions. Reset clears the dashboard session but does not itself revoke the extension permission. Clear site storage to erase the saved extension ID.

Limits

URL queries and fragments are removed; titles may contain private information. Quoted conversation content that is not marked cannot always be identified. Keyword classifiers are incomplete, uncertain proxies, not validated personality assessments. No sensitive trait inference is intended or supported. Outbound product links open retailer websites with their own policies.

Questions

Report software issues through the project issue tracker. Do not include personal records in public issues.
